## Revision 2 — strategic communications review

The initial presentation retained the report’s diagnosis but assumed familiarity with the Action Plan, underplayed its private-sector origin and softened its sovereign-AI purpose into broad collaboration language.

The revised sequence explains the January 2025 plan and its fifty accepted recommendations before presenting the assessment. It separates the government’s Action Plan from the Project SAIL consortium’s implementation review and from the government AI Taskforce addressed by the recommendations. The website presents the review at nineteen months, not as a live assessment.

The opening now identifies the report as a contribution by private sector leaders to sovereign AI leadership. Its direct quotation on shifting from government teams to the whole nation comes from the foreword. The closing offer, coordination-ceiling quotation and statement about the state setting direction are also drawn directly from the report. Brief editorial headings are not styled as direct quotations.

The Compact includes the report’s important limitation: capture is the most fragile part of the circle. The recommendations start with building well; lasting value capture and distribution are continuing work, not promised results of a twelve-month programme. Recommendation headings have been brought closer to the source. No new policy claims, outcomes, taskforce membership or endorsements are invented.

Visual direction: a white base, ink and cobalt, teal and rust reserved for evidence categories, a geometric SAIL wordmark, a left reading rail, and finite motion tied to information. The original slogan, slash mark, italic serif display type and large dark backgrounds have been removed.
